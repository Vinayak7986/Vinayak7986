## ⏱️ 5-Minute Execution Checklist

Follow these steps to upgrade your profile:

1. **Update Intro & Headline:**
   * Go to your LinkedIn profile: [linkedin.com/in/vinayak-more-b37309264/](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vinayak-more-b37309264/)
   * Click the pencil icon ✏️ next to your name.
   * Copy the recommended headline from [01_HEADLINE_AND_ABOUT.md](./01_HEADLINE_AND_ABOUT.md#option-1-executive--balanced-recommended) and paste it.
   * Add your custom button/link pointing to your portfolio: `https://vinayakmore.netlify.app/`.

2. **Update About Section:**
   * Scroll to the **About** section and click ✏️.
   * Paste the complete narrative from [01_HEADLINE_AND_ABOUT.md](./01_HEADLINE_AND_ABOUT.md#section-2-about-section-character-limit-2600).

3. **Update Work Experience:**
   * Scroll down to **Experience** > Click **+** or edit your **Ambiguity Labs** role.
   * Paste the STAR bullet points from [02_EXPERIENCE_AND_LEADERSHIP.md](./02_EXPERIENCE_AND_LEADERSHIP.md#experience-entry-1-ambiguity-labs).

4. **Pin Featured Cards:**
   * Click **Add profile section** > **Recommended** > **Add Featured** > **Add a link (+)**.
   * Add the 4 links from [03_PROJECTS_AND_FEATURED.md](./03_PROJECTS_AND_FEATURED.md#part-1-featured-section-pin-to-top-of-linkedin-profile) (Portfolio, AskAI, PLC Dashboard, GitHub).
